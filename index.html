A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated shopping list used to organize product references for a Kakobuy shopping workflow. Entries may include links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. The term does not guarantee that a list is official, current or independently verified.

02 / A practical browsing method

Turn a long list
into a useful shortlist.

Use this three-step approach whether you are comparing a few everyday pieces or organizing a larger Kakobuy shopping list.

01.

Choose a purpose

Start with the category and occasion: everyday shoes, a warm layer, a training jersey or a small accessory. Set a budget before comparing individual listings.

02.

Compare the evidence

Check the size chart, material description and available detail photos. Record what is missing. A polished image or familiar logo is not a substitute for product information.

03.

Calculate the full cost

Where applicable, include domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and import costs. Recheck price and availability at the source before you pay.

Product comparison checklist
Category Record these details Why it matters
Shoes Model reference, foot-length guidance, size system, upper and sole materials. Size labels and intended uses can differ between models.
Tops & layers Chest width, length, shoulder width, sleeve length and fabric composition. Measurements help you compare the item with clothing you already own.
Pants & shorts Waist range, rise, inseam, leg opening and stretch information. A waist size alone does not describe the overall fit.
Headwear Circumference range, closure type, crown shape and materials. Adjustability and shape affect comfort as well as appearance.
Jewelry & accessories Dimensions, weight where stated, material composition and fastening type. Close-up photographs can make scale and finish difficult to judge.
Every item Source link, date checked, current price, shipping estimate and return terms. Saved shopping lists can become outdated as listings change.

06 / Straight answers

open another tab.

Clear answers to common questions about shopping lists, product comparisons and this CAFANS guide.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated shopping list used to organize product references for a Kakobuy shopping workflow. Entries may include links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. The term does not guarantee that a list is official, current or independently verified.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent CAFANS browsing guide with category links. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet and is not presented as an official Kakobuy service.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare foot-length guidance, size conversions and available detail photos. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric information and the intended fit. A size label alone is not enough.

Does a product photo prove brand authenticity?

No. A photo, logo or brand name alone does not establish authenticity. Check the seller's identity, provenance, product documentation and applicable return terms. Editorial imagery on this page is not proof of available branded inventory.

Is the listed item price the total delivered cost?

Not necessarily. Where applicable, add domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, taxes and duties. Confirm current amounts and destination rules before paying.

Where do the links on this page go?

Category buttons open their corresponding CAFANS category pages. All other navigation and shopping links lead to the CAFANS homepage.
